id	name	desc
1	Plant Battle	Alliances can fight for the Circus at Level 4 and the Playground at Level 5. Once occupied, all alliance members can claim rewards.
2	Alliance Championship	Held weekly with generous rewards, becoming the champion of the Alliance Tournament is the goal of every alliance.
3	Alliance Raid	Initiated by the Alliance Leader or Co-Leader. Defeat zombies for rewards, and clear chapters to earn ranking rewards.
4	Alliance Support	Alliance members can support each other's towns and fend off zombies together.
5	Mine Bonus	Alliance members mining the same mine will receive a bonus (+5% coin production per additional member).
6	Alliance Skills	Join an alliance to activate and upgrade alliance skills. They become inactive upon leaving the alliance.
7	Alliance Garden	Alliance members can fertilize in the Alliance Garden every day to increase Alliance EXP and earn personal contribution and stamina.
